About Magic Home Lighting

Crafting Light, Inspiring Spaces

Magic Home Lighting is a premier manufacturer and exporter of decorative lighting based in China. Since 2010, we have specialized in designing and producing high-quality crystal chandeliers, LED lighting fixtures, and custom lighting solutions for the global market.

Our products are crafted with precision using premium K9 crystals, stainless steel, and energy-efficient LED technology. We serve wholesalers, retailers, interior designers, and project contractors across Europe, Africa, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.
